2. Booking and payment

3. Cancellation by the guest

  • Up to 15 days before arrival: free cancellation with a full refund to the card used (usually within 5–10 business days).

  • Early departure: no refund for unused nights.
  • To cancel, email info@petitenest.gr or contact us on +30 698 038 4084 (phone call, WhatsApp or Viber), quoting your booking code. The cancellation date is the day we receive your request.
  • Date changes: only subject to availability and after agreeing with us.

4. Cancellation by us

If the apartment cannot be provided for reasons beyond our control (e.g. damage, force majeure, double booking), we will refund the full amount. Our liability is limited to the amount paid.

5. Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 15:00 · Check-out by 11:00. Early check-in or late check-out only by prior arrangement.

6. Guests

  • Maximum 3 guests. Only the guests included in the booking may stay overnight.

  • Daytime visitors are welcome, but may not stay overnight.

7. House rules

  • Smoking is allowed only on the balcony; it is not permitted inside the apartment.
  • No parties or events. Quiet hours 23:00–08:00, out of respect for our neighbours.
  • No pets.
  • In case of a serious breach of these rules, the stay may be ended without a refund.

  • Guests are responsible for any damage caused during their stay beyond normal wear and tear, and must cover the cost of repair.
  • We are not responsible for the loss or theft of personal belongings.
  • Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising children, especially on the balcony.

9. Personal data

Your details (name, email, phone, dates of stay) are used only to manage your booking and to communicate with you. We use Stripe for payments and Resend for sending emails. We never share your details with third parties for marketing. To access, correct or delete your data, email us at info@petitenest.gr.

These terms are governed by Greek law. The courts of Piraeus have jurisdiction over any dispute.
