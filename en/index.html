FAQ

Good to know before you arrive

Where is Petite Nest located?

In Mikrolimano, Piraeus, just 20 metres from the marina, with seaside cafés and restaurants a few steps away.

How do I get there from the airport?

Athens International Airport “Eleftherios Venizelos” is about 42 km away, around 55 minutes by taxi or car.

How far is Piraeus port?

Piraeus Metro station, next to the port, is about 2.5 km away. A handy base before or after the islands.

Is there parking?

The apartment does not currently have private parking.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00 and check-out by 11:00, with self check-in.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 3 guests, with 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom.

Is there Wi-Fi and a workspace?

Yes, fast Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace, great for remote work too.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed.
